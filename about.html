A LITTLE CONTEXT

About me.

I’m a Cybersecurity student at MIVA Open University. I’m building my knowledge and exploring the different roles and challenges in the field.

Why cybersecurity?

I have always loved technology, which is one reason I chose to study cybersecurity. Our world is increasingly digital, and the systems we rely on can be exposed to threats. Cybersecurity helps protect those systems and keep them safer.

Learning with purpose

I’m developing my skills through regular study and practice. I’m particularly interested in how organizations assess risk and strengthen their security.

Where I’m headed

I’m interested in becoming a cybersecurity analyst and an ethical hacker, with the skills to help identify security weaknesses and strengthen defenses. I’m also interested in incident response and governance, risk, and compliance.

Beyond the screen

Outside my studies, I enjoy reading books and exploring technology and cybersecurity, practising coding, and staying active through sports and fitness. These interests help me keep learning, stay curious, and maintain balance.
