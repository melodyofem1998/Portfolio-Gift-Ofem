PROJECT CONCEPT 05 · INCIDENT RESPONSE

Account incident guide.

A calm first-steps checklist for when you think an online account may have been accessed by someone else.

Start safely: If you suspect your device is compromised, use a trusted device to secure the account. For financial accounts, contact your bank through its official app or phone number.

KEEP A CLEAR HEAD

Small steps can help regain control.

Use the official recovery instructions for the affected service. If you see unauthorized financial activity, contact your bank or payment provider directly through a trusted channel.

✳

Never share your password or one-time verification codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
