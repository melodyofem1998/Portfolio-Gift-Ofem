Strength: waiting for a sample 0 / 5

Start typing a fictional sample to get suggestions.

  • Use at least 12 characters.
  • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters.
  • Add a number.
  • Consider a symbol.
  • Avoid common words and repeated characters.

WHAT THIS DEMO CHECKS

Longer. Less predictable. Unique.

This learning demo gives a simple estimate based on length and character variety. It is not a guarantee of account security and does not compare your sample with leaked-password lists.

✳

A long, unique passphrase is often easier to remember than a short password with small substitutions.
