PROJECT CONCEPT 02 · SECURITY AWARENESS

Phishing awareness guide.

Practise noticing common warning signs in a fictional message before you click, reply, or share information.

FICTIONAL MESSAGE EXAMPLE

SAMPLE INBOX

Action required: account review

From
Account Team <account-support@signin-alert.example>
To
Student
Subject
Action required within 24 hours

Hello valued user,

We noticed an issue with your account. Your access may be paused today. Confirm your details now to keep using your account.

Review your account

Thank you,
Account Team

This is a made-up example. The sample link is not clickable.

YOUR TURN

What feels unusual?

Take a moment to inspect the sender, wording, and request. Then reveal the clues.

Warning signs in this example

  • Unfamiliar sender domain: the address doesn’t clearly match a known organization.
  • Pressure to act quickly: the message uses a short deadline to rush a decision.
  • Generic greeting: “Hello valued user” doesn’t identify the recipient.
  • Asks you to confirm details: don’t share passwords or sensitive information through an unexpected message.
  • Unexpected link: verify through the organization’s official app or a website address you type yourself.
✳

If you’re unsure, don’t use the message’s link or reply address. Contact the organization through a trusted channel.
